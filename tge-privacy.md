# Privacy policy: Tab Group Essentials

_Last updated: 2026-10-25_

Tab Group Essentials is a Microsoft Edge extension that lets you save your browser tab groups and reopen them later from a side panel.

## What the extension accesses

To show and save your tab groups, the extension reads, for the tabs that are in tab groups:

- the page title, web address (URL) and icon (favicon) of each tab, and
- the name, color and order of each tab group.

This is browsing information, so it is treated as personal information in this policy.

## What is stored, and where

The extension also stores your settings (on/off preferences from its settings page) on your device.

When you click **Save** on a group (or on a single tab), the extension stores that group's name, color, the icon you chose, and the title, address and favicon of its saved tabs. This data is kept **only on your device**, in the extension's own local storage in Microsoft Edge. Groups and tabs you have not saved are shown in the side panel but are never stored.

## What leaves your device

Nothing. The extension has no server, no account and no analytics. It does not send data anywhere, does not load remote code, and does not share data with anyone. The only network requests are the ones Microsoft Edge itself makes to show the tab icons that websites provide.

## Your control

- Saving is always your choice: nothing is saved until you click Save.
- **Remove group** deletes a saved group from storage; the **X** on a tab deletes that tab from the saved group.
- Removing the extension from Microsoft Edge deletes all data it stored.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
| --- | --- |
| `tabs` | Read the title, address and icon of tabs in groups; open, focus and close tabs when you click them in the panel. |
| `tabGroups` | Read the name and color of groups, and rename, recolor and recreate groups when you ask. |
| `storage` | Keep your saved groups on your device. |
| `sidePanel` | Show the extension in the browser side panel. |
